## 9. Cybersecurity

9.1 The Startup shall host pilot data in India, encrypt it in transit and at rest, and restrict access to named
personnel.
9.2 The Startup shall report any security incident affecting pilot data to the Department within 24 hours of
becoming aware of it.

## 10. Exit and termination

10.1 Either party may terminate on 30 days' written notice if the other materially breaches this agreement and
fails to remedy the breach within 15 days of notice.
10.2 On termination the Startup is paid for milestones already accepted under clause 5.1, and nothing more.
10.3 On termination or expiry the Startup shall hand over pilot-generated data per clause 7 and delete Department
data it holds, confirming deletion in writing.
10.4 A termination and its reason are recorded in the platform's failure registry for learning across
departments. This record is internal and is not a debarment.
