# Data Ownership & IP Clause Set

Two variants. The API inserts exactly one `## VARIANT:` section into clause 7–8 of the pilot agreement, chosen
from the agreement's data-sensitivity level. Everything above the first `## VARIANT:` heading is guidance and is
never inserted.

Default position (research brief §7): the Startup keeps its core IP; the Department owns its own data and gets a
non-exclusive licence to what the pilot produces; citizen PII is handled only as consented.

## VARIANT: STANDARD

## 7. Data ownership

7.1 The Department owns all baseline and operational data it provides ("Department Data").
7.2 Data generated at the pilot site during the pilot ("Pilot Data") is owned jointly. The Department has a
perpetual, royalty-free, non-exclusive licence to use Pilot Data for public purposes, including sharing it with
other government departments.
7.3 The Startup shall export all Pilot Data in an open, machine-readable format (CSV or JSON) within 14 days of
the end of the pilot.
7.4 No personal data of citizens is expected to be processed in this pilot. If that changes, the parties shall
move to the SENSITIVE_PII clause set by written amendment before any such data is collected.

## 8. Intellectual property

8.1 The Startup retains all rights in its pre-existing IP and in improvements to its core product.
8.2 The Department receives a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use the pilot deliverables for the
duration of the pilot and any extension.
8.3 Nothing in this agreement obliges the Department to purchase further licences. Any scale-up will be a separate
procurement decision.

## VARIANT: SENSITIVE_PII

## 7. Data ownership and personal data

7.1 The Department owns all baseline and operational data it provides ("Department Data").
7.2 Data generated at the pilot site during the pilot ("Pilot Data") is owned jointly. The Department has a
perpetual, royalty-free, non-exclusive licence to use Pilot Data for public purposes.
7.3 **Personal data.** Personal data of citizens may be processed only (a) as covered by a consent notice approved
by the Department before collection, (b) in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and
(c) with the Startup acting solely on the Department's documented instructions.
7.4 The Startup shall not use personal data to train models, build profiles or for any purpose outside this pilot.
7.5 Personal data shall be stored only in India, and shall be deleted or returned within 30 days of the end of the
pilot, with written confirmation.
7.6 Biometric data shall not be collected under this agreement.
7.7 The Startup shall export all non-personal Pilot Data in an open, machine-readable format within 14 days of the
end of the pilot.

## 8. Intellectual property

8.1 The Startup retains all rights in its pre-existing IP and in improvements to its core product.
8.2 The Department receives a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use the pilot deliverables for the
duration of the pilot and any extension.
8.3 Nothing in this agreement obliges the Department to purchase further licences. Any scale-up will be a separate
procurement decision.
